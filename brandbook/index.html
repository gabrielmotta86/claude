Logo Misuse

To maintain brand integrity, avoid the following:

Stretched

Do not stretch or distort

Rotated

Do not rotate

Recolored

Do not use unapproved colors

Low Contrast

Do not place on low-contrast backgrounds

Effects

Do not add shadows or effects

Crowded

Do not crowd with other elements

Photography & Imagery Style

Do

  • Use natural lighting whenever possible
  • Feature authentic, candid moments
  • Maintain consistent color grading
  • Ensure diverse representation

Don't

  • Use overly staged or stock-looking images
  • Apply heavy filters that distort brand colors
  • Use low-resolution or pixelated images
  • Crop logos or key brand elements
