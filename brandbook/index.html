04

Brand Guidelines

Rules and principles that govern how /NGAGE is expressed visually and verbally across every touchpoint.

Brand Voice & Tone

Bold

/NGAGE communicates with confidence and clarity. We lead conversations, not follow them. Every statement should feel decisive and direct.

Elevated

Like the alpine peaks that inspire us, our language aims higher. We choose precision over filler. Quality over quantity. Every word earns its place.

Forward

The "/" in /NGAGE means movement. Our voice is future-facing, action-oriented, and always pushing toward the next summit.

Grounded

Ambition without pretension. We're bold but never hollow. Our voice is rooted in substance, expertise, and authenticity.

Photography & Imagery Style

Do

  • Favor golden-hour and dramatic natural lighting
  • Use high-contrast, cinematic compositions
  • Feature expansive landscapes and textured surfaces
  • Maintain warm-to-cool tonal balance
  • Show depth through atmospheric perspective and haze

Don't

  • Use flat, evenly-lit stock photography
  • Apply heavy filters that distort the brand palette
  • Use low-resolution or heavily compressed images
  • Default to generic corporate or tech imagery
  • Use saturated, candy-colored or neon aesthetics

Graphic Elements & Patterns

Diagonal Lines — derived from the "/" slash

Geometric Grid — structural backbone

Gradient Texture — atmospheric depth

Iconography

Stroke Weight 2px
Corner Radius 1px
Grid Size 24 × 24px
Style Outlined, geometric

Icons follow a sharp, geometric style with consistent 2px strokes. Rounded caps are avoided to match the angular energy of the /NGAGE logomark. Place icon files in assets/icons/.
